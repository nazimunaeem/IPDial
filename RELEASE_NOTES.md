# DialDuo Release Notes

## v2.0.0

### Highlights
- **DialDuo Rebrand & Logo**: New adaptive launcher icon and two-tone **DialDuo** wordmark across the app.
- **Redesigned Contacts Tab**: Clean contact rows showing avatar, name, and dial button, with inline card expansion for multi-number contacts.
- **Dedicated Contact Details Screen**: Full contact view with photo, labeled phone numbers, copy/dialpad/SMS actions, auto-detected third-party calling apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Meet, etc.), system Contacts editor integration, and confirmed in-app deletion.
- **Instant Contact Sync**: Real-time contact provider observer and foreground resume sync so newly added or edited contacts appear right away.
- **SMS & History Quick Actions**: Send SMS directly from Contact Details, the call history long-press menu, and the single-line action bar in the call history detail dialog.
- **SIM Call Proximity & Permissions**: Fixed proximity sensor wake lock during SIM calls, removed unnecessary restricted permission prompts on launch, and added an interactive live dialer preview in Dialpad Style settings.
