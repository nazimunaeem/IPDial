Everything you need to call

Crystal-clear SIP audio

Noise cancellation, codec management, and smart audio routing keep every call sharp.

Rotary keypad & smart dial

A classic rotary dial design with T9 contact search and one-tap call from suggestions.

Multiple SIP accounts

Manage several accounts with per-account balance, status, and Pro management.

Pro features

Call recording, device-slot management, and a points system — all managed in-app.

Call log & recordings

Full activity log with recordings (Pro), plus favorites and most-called shortcuts.

Privacy first

SIP credentials and call history stay on your device — nothing is synced, no tracking, no ads.
